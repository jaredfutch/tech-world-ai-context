# Community Code of Conduct

The Tech-World AI Context Framework welcomes useful, respectful participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, tools, and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and participants should:

- discuss ideas, implementations, and disagreements respectfully;
- critique the work rather than attacking the person;
- provide reproducible evidence when reporting technical problems;
- respect privacy, intellectual property, licensing, and security boundaries;
- keep private credentials, customer information, personal data, and confidential project material out of public issues and pull requests;
- disclose relevant limitations, assumptions, and conflicts of interest when they materially affect a proposal;
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, spam, impersonation, or knowingly misleading claims.

## Project integrity

Do not use the Tech-World name or project channels to imply endorsement of an unofficial implementation. See `TRADEMARKS.md` for the branding boundary.

Security concerns that could expose credentials, private data, or exploitable details should follow `SECURITY.md` rather than being posted publicly.

## Moderation

Tech-World LLC, as maintainer of the canonical upstream repository, may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions or participation that materially violate these expectations, repository policies, applicable law, or platform rules. Repeated or serious violations may result in participation restrictions.

Good-faith disagreement, competing technical proposals, and respectful criticism are welcome and are not violations of this policy.

## Scope

This policy applies to participation in the canonical repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed community spaces.
