# Compatibility

Third-party implementations may use factual compatibility language when they satisfy the mandatory requirements in `specification-v1.md`.

## Allowed compatibility statement

A conforming implementation may state:

> Compatible with Tech-World AI Context Framework v1

That statement means the implementation follows the mandatory v1 requirements. It does not mean Tech-World LLC reviewed, certified, endorsed, sponsored, operates, or supports the implementation.

## Do not use misleading language

Without separate written permission from Tech-World LLC, do not describe a third-party implementation as:

- official Tech-World software;
- Tech-World certified;
- Tech-World approved;
- endorsed or sponsored by Tech-World LLC;
- operated or supported by Tech-World LLC.

## Forks

Forks may preserve license attribution and may accurately describe their origin. Independent forks should use their own product branding.

## Future compatibility tests

The project may introduce automated conformance checks in a future minor release. Until then, compatibility is self-attested against the published specification.

False or misleading claims may be challenged by the maintainers, but publication in this repository does not create a certification program or warranty.
